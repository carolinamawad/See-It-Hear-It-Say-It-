🎵 Sing Along!

Sing these fun songs with your class! 🎤

🌈 The Color Song

Red, red, I see red! 🔴  Blue, blue, the sky is blue! 🔵
Yellow sunshine shining bright ☀️  Green, green the trees at night! 🌳
Orange like a yummy fruit 🍊  Purple grapes are so cute! 🍇
Colors, colors all around — colors, colors can be found! 🌈

🐾 The Animal Song

🐱 The cat says meow!  🐶 The dog says woof!
🐸 The frog goes ribbit!  🐄 The cow goes moo!
🦁 The lion goes roar!  🐘 The elephant stomps too!
All the animals make their sound — animals, animals all around! 🐾

🔢 The Number Song

One, two, three — clap with me! 👏
Four, five, six — pick up sticks! 🪵
Seven, eight, nine — everything's fine! ✨
Ten, eleven, twelve — books on the shelf! 📚
Thirteen to twenty — that's plenty! 🎉

🍎 The Food Song

🍎 Apple, apple, red and round!  🍌 Banana, yellow found!
🍊 Orange, juicy and sweet!  🍓 Strawberry, yummy treat!
🥛 Milk, milk, good for me!  🍞 Bread, bread, yum yum yum!
Eat your food and grow up strong — sing the food song all day long! 💪

👁️ My Body Song

Head and shoulders, knees and toes!
Eyes and ears and mouth and nose!
Hands go clap and feet go stomp!
Touch your tummy, give a jump! 🎉
